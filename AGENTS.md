# Final-answer maintenance rules

- Keep model input separate from audit storage. UUIDs, hashes, local paths, deployment metadata and provider diagnostics do not belong in answer prompts. Use source timestamps for observations and short source-local subject aliases where cross-frame relationships matter. Never merge observations just because their timestamps match.
- Document the input/output field contract and the reason for each retained field before changing it. Report measured bytes and tokens during authorized input preparation; "compression implemented" does not establish that every pending question fits. Audit the full question inventory, not only the latest recovery selection.
- Keep original task scope separate from a recovery's selected questions. Join all original questions with saved sources and cumulative dispatch state; a previous candidate list is not the complete pending inventory. Score saved outcomes without turning unstarted, capacity-blocked siblings into model failures, and label every subset denominator explicitly.
- The human requested removal of branch hash computations and checks on 2026-10-19. Do not add new hash gates. Preserve historical results and paid-request state; migrate active identity consumers together rather than deleting a producer and leaving broken readers. The human then approved retiring signed webhooks and customer automatic updates: remove their hash implementations and reject their entry points before side effects. Do not fall back to unsigned delivery or unverified installation. This decision does not retire official checkpoint loading or maintainer-operated deployment tools, and those separate protocols must not become a new evaluation-resume gate. State the remaining scope instead of claiming the whole repository is hash-free.
- Final answers follow the original question: an option, number, timestamp, time interval, or short text. Do not require explanations, copied observations, or generated evidence-reference lists. Keep audit provenance locally. Fix an unnecessarily verbose response contract before increasing the output budget.
- Input compression and output truncation are separate problems. Reuse the existing lossless codecs; preserve every observation, explicit negative, unknown, timestamp, identity relation, and source ordering. Do not silently import historical lossy fallbacks.
- Preserve the cumulative request ledger and historical answers. A new prompt or encoding must not bypass a previously dispatched answer, including an invalid response or unknown receipt. Do not change scoring to salvage truncated replies without explicit authorization.
- Tests, test edits, lint, type checks, syntax checks, builds, and health probes require explicit task-specific human authorization. Read-only source and diff review is permitted. Do not claim tests passed when they were not run.
- Code, prompts, comments, published documentation, and commit messages are English. Preserve original questions, options, matching terms, observations, and answers verbatim.
